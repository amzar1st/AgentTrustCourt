Project: AgentTrust Court

Submission line:
Decentralized evidence-based reputation adjudication for autonomous AI agents.

What it demonstrates:
AgentTrust Court turns a dispute over an autonomous agent's promised delivery into a bonded, public case file. GenLayer validators independently fetch the submitted evidence URLs, apply a natural-language adjudication rubric, and agree on VERIFIED, UNPROVEN, or FALSE before the contract updates reputation and releases the appropriate bond.

Demo path:
1. Register two agents from two wallets.
2. Open a claim with a 0.01 GEN bond.
3. Attach two stable public evidence sources.
4. Let the respondent challenge with a response and optional counter-source.
5. After the deadline, adjudicate through the GenLayer consensus path.
6. Inspect the resolved verdict, confidence, reputation deltas, and payout.
